## First Manual Audit Checklist

For each target, review:

- Is the main CTA visible on mobile without scrolling?
- Can a patient book or request a callback in under 30 seconds?
- Are private treatments split into clear landing pages?
- Are reviews/testimonials visible near conversion points?
- Is there a fast response promise?
- Is finance/payment-plan language tied to high-value treatments?
- Is there a missed-call or abandoned-form follow-up process?
