# Video Assets

This directory contains video assets for the Elite Automations marketing site.

## Required Videos

### Background Videos
- `atmosphere-tech.mp4` - Technology atmosphere background video for hero section (looping, slow motion)

### Film Showcase Videos
- `film-automation.mp4` - Automation demonstration video
- `film-success.mp4` - Client success stories video
- `film-tour.mp4` - Platform walkthrough video

### Thumbnails (optional)
- `film-automation-thumb.jpg`
- `film-success-thumb.jpg`
- `film-tour-thumb.jpg`

## Video Specifications

- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 minimum
- Frame Rate: 30fps or 60fps
- Bitrate: High quality (5-10 Mbps)
- Audio: Optional for showcase films, muted for background

## Notes

These videos should be generated using Remotion or sourced from stock footage.
The atmosphere-tech video should have a modern, technological feel with particles, code, or abstract tech visuals.
